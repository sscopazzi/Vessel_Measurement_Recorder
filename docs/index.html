Things to try

  • Screen or B: the next screen. Hold Screen for 1 s: the knob picks an action (KNOB SEL), let go to do it.
  • Mark: a tap makes a location mark; held 1.5 s it starts or stops a cast.
  • Hold Screen (its hold toggle) and tap Mark: the 7-segment steps to its next reading.
  • Knob: drag it, scroll on it or use the arrow keys (the brightness, or the action while Screen is held).
  • Pick CTD cast with Niskin bottles, then press Start cast on the WTB. Bottles 1-3 fire at their planned depths; hold 4-6 to fire them by hand.
  • Run the battery down at ×60 to see the 3.30 V shutdown. It stays off until you switch the power off and on (charge it first with USB power).
