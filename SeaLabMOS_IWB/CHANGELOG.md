# SeaLabMOS IWB changelog

Newest first. Hundredths for small changes, tenths for larger ones.

- **1.11** (21 Nov 2026): The `$SLH` supply voltage is the smoothed one `$SLF` uses.
- **1.1** (18 Nov 2026): Niskin bottles: the 6318 solenoid driver, the firing capacitor on A5, `$CMD,FIRE` and the `$SLF` line.
- **1.01** (14 Nov 2026): Fixes.
- **1.0** (14 Nov 2026): First release of the wired build: Ethernet, 5 Hz sampling, TEOS-10 salinity and density, 30-min replay buffer, firmware over Ethernet or its own Wi-Fi.
