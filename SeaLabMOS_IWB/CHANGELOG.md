# SeaLabMOS IWB changelog

Newest first. From 2.0.0, [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org) (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH), as set out under Versions in `README.md`. Up to 1.11, hundredths for small changes, tenths for larger ones.

- **2.0.0** (26 Nov 2026): Numbered 2.0.0 with the WTB, whose link to the VMR changed; the IWB itself works as before and its link to the WTB is unchanged. Update it before the WTB (see the WTB's 2.0.0). Licence headers (GPL-3.0-or-later with the GSW additional permission).
- **1.11** (21 Nov 2026): The `$SLH` supply voltage is the smoothed one `$SLF` uses.
- **1.1** (18 Nov 2026): Niskin bottles: the 6318 solenoid driver, the firing capacitor on A5, `$CMD,FIRE` and the `$SLF` line.
- **1.01** (14 Nov 2026): Fixes.
- **1.0** (14 Nov 2026): First release of the wired build: Ethernet, 5 Hz sampling, TEOS-10 salinity and density, 30-min replay buffer, firmware over Ethernet or its own Wi-Fi.
